● five themes

Research themes.

We build methods, then apply them. Each theme is a method we maintain alongside disease-specific applications.

01 — multi-omics integration

Tensor and graph methods that combine genomic, transcriptomic and proteomic layers without forcing them into a single embedding. We report — not hide — the places where layers disagree.

02 — sex as a biological variable

Disease trajectories modeled as they actually differ across sex. Sex-stratified factor models, sex-aware deconvolution of bulk transcriptomes, and tools to audit existing pooled studies for missed sex effects.

03 — interpretable models

If a method can't be explained to a clinician, it isn't finished. We work on regularized, sparse and graph-structured predictors whose individual terms carry biological meaning.

04 — longitudinal disease

Most omics studies are cross-sectional. We work on tensor decompositions and trajectory models that accommodate sparse, irregular sampling typical of clinical neurodegenerative cohorts.

05 — open methods

All methods are released as documented R / Python packages with reproducible benchmarking pipelines. → resources
